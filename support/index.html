FAQ

My puzzle isn't listed. Can I add it? Yes. Scan the barcode or tap Add a puzzle, then enter the title, brand and piece count. Once it's added, anyone can find it.

Something about a puzzle is wrong. Open the puzzle, tap ⋯, then Suggest a correction.

Does the timer keep running if I close the app? Yes. If it runs for 4 hours or more, you'll be asked to keep or adjust the time. You can also edit past sessions.

Who can see my puzzles? Only you. Puzzles you add to Browse puzzles are public, without your name.

How do I report a puzzle or photo? Open the puzzle, tap ⋯, then Report.

How do I export my data or delete my account? Settings → Your data → Export my data, or Settings → Account → Sign-in → Delete account.
