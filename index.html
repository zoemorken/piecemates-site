A journal for your jigsaw puzzles

Keep track of the puzzles you own, the ones you want to try, and every one you finish.

  • Add a puzzle by scanning its barcode or searching for it.
  • Keep your puzzles in Want to Try, Working On, Completed and Own.
  • Time your builds. The timer keeps running even if you close the app.
  • Log each finished puzzle with a photo, ratings and notes.
  • Set a yearly goal and see your stats.

Coming soon to iPhone. Your journal is private; puzzles you add to the shared list never show who added them.
